Notes
-----
* Bioconductor packages (Seurat, SeuratObject, fgsea, ComplexHeatmap, circlize)
  were installed via BiocManager against Bioconductor 3.21 (matching R 4.5).
* setup.R installs any missing packages automatically at run time, so exact
  versions on a fresh machine may differ slightly. For byte-for-byte
  reproducibility, pin the versions above (e.g. with renv::init() then
  renv::install("Seurat@5.5.0"), etc.).
* To capture the exact environment on YOUR machine after a run, execute:
      writeLines(capture.output(sessionInfo()), "session_info_local.txt")
